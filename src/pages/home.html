Why it is built this way

Your words, not ours

The tools work from the vocabulary and sentences you are already teaching, so the practice matches the lesson.

Minutes, not evenings

Each tool exists to save you time, so making a resource takes a few minutes instead of an evening. No accounts needed for the free tools.

Ready when it is right

Tools are tried in real classrooms before they go on the site. New ones are added when they are ready, not before.
